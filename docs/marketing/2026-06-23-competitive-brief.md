# WheelTodo — Competitive Marketing Brief

**Product:** WheelTodo — "Spin. Focus. Done." Add tasks → spin a wheel → it randomly picks ONE → instantly launches a Pomodoro focus timer. Built to kill decision paralysis and task-initiation friction. Key differentiator: **Rest Mode** (rest activities protect your streak instead of breaking it). Gentle, anti-hustle, ADHD-friendly brand. Offline-first, no account required. Platforms: iOS + Android (Expo) + web. Pre-beta, global English-first launch.

**Status:** Pre-beta marketing/positioning research. **Date:** 2026-06-23.

**Method & confidence note.** This brief is built from ~80 live web searches/fetches across five research threads (June 2026). Product mechanics, pricing, and platforms are well-cited from primary sources (company sites, app stores). **Social follower counts are the weakest data** — most come from third-party aggregators/review snippets, not audited platform reads, and they drift. Treat all follower/download figures as directional, not precise. Where a claim is my inference rather than a citation, it is flagged inline as *(inferred)*. Sources are cited inline as `[domain, year]` and listed in full under References.

## 2. White space

**Is "gentle / anti-hustle / ADHD-friendly / rest-positive" under-served?** Partly. The *gentle ADHD* lane has strong incumbents — Tiimo ("nothing turns red"), Finch (self-care bird), Llama Life ("anti-hustle"), Focus Bear ("gentle coach"). So WheelTodo cannot claim to invent gentle productivity. **But two specific positions are genuinely open:**

1. **"Rest protects your streak" as a first-class, positive action — unoccupied.** Streak-preservation exists in habit trackers, but always framed as *damage control*: "streak shields" (HabitStreak), "your streak pauses instead of resetting" (Habi), "skip days" (Streaks, Way of Life) [habitstreak.me / habi.app, 2026]. Every one treats rest as an *exemption* ("don't worry, you didn't lose it"). **No app found frames rest as a move you actively make that *earns/protects* streak status.** WheelTodo's Rest Mode + Drained/Okay/Restless energy check-in inverts the category default. This is the single most ownable, on-brand, on-trend differentiator.

2. **"Decision paralysis" as a branded position — under-claimed.** The concept appears in app *copy* (Morgen markets its planner against "decision paralysis") but almost no app is *named/branded* around it [morgen.so, 2026]. High intent, on-mechanic, low competition.

**Is "spin a wheel to beat decision paralysis" novel?** **No — and the brief must not over-claim it.** Generic decision wheels are a saturated novelty category (Tiny Decisions, Picker Wheel, Spin The Wheel, Wheel Decide) and several even market a "to-do list" wheel use case [spinthewheel.app, 2024]. There are **two apps literally named "Task Roulette"** — but both are *group/team task-assigners*, not solo focus tools [play.google.com / monday.com, 2024]. A "Random task" Todoist Chrome extension picks a task at random (a button, not a wheel, no timer) [softonic, 2024]. **"Pomodoro wheel" is essentially not a product** — searches surface Pomodoro apps whose *circular timer dial* is loosely called a "wheel," not spin-to-randomize selectors [focuskeeper.co, 2026].

**Direct mechanic clones:** the two "Task Roulette" apps (team-oriented, no Pomodoro) and the Todoist "Random task" extension are the closest. **None combines wheel → ONE task → auto-launched Pomodoro → gentle/ADHD framing → Rest Mode.** That *bundle* is the white space — the differentiator is the integration, not any single piece. A community ADHD writer independently endorses decision-wheels as a legitimate task-initiation strategy ("being handed a single task to start is a legitimate strategy"), validating the core insight [substack.com, 2025].

## 3. Short-form marketing patterns that work for this category

**Scale of the opportunity.** #ADHD has 6B+ views on TikTok; #productivitytips 112M+ [washingtonpost.com, 2022]. Critically, **first-person peer content outperforms clinical/educational content** — "someone saying 'this is my life' rather than 'here are the symptoms' produces faster attitude change" [neurolaunch.com, n.d.]. Lead with relatable POV, not feature explainers.

**Named, viral content tropes to build pillars around (all citable):**
- **"ADHD tax"** — hidden cost of executive dysfunction (late fees, re-buying lost stuff) [additudemag.com, 2024]. Hook: "WheelTodo saves you the ADHD tax of never starting."
- **"Dopamine menu"** — from a How to ADHD video; appetizers/mains/sides framework [theconversation.com, 2024]. WheelTodo's wheel *is* a dopamine-delivery mechanic; Rest Mode = the "desserts."
- **"Body doubling"** — biggest ADHD hack on TikTok; people go live doing tasks [add.org, 2026]. (Relevant to WheelTodo's future companion, less so now.)
- **"Romanticize your tasks" / "study with me"** — aesthetic-driven, massive recurring formats [tiktok.com, n.d.]. Fits WheelTodo's pretty, cozy UI.

**Format mechanics that demonstrably raise performance:**
- **Subtitles are near-universal in top ADHD content** — two input channels, better retention [neurolaunch.com, n.d.].
- High contrast, bold text callouts, rapid cuts, clear narrative arc to lower cognitive load [neurolaunch.com, n.d.].
- **Short clips (0–10s) hit ~81% completion on TikTok** [socialpilot.co, 2026] — favoring satisfying-loop demos.

**Reel formats with proven category fit (concrete):**
1. **"Watch the wheel decide for me" satisfying-loop demo** — the spin → pick → timer-start is inherently a short, satisfying, screen-native clip. This is exactly the lane Structured wins in (its timeline-fill *is* the content) [tiktok.com, 2026]; WheelTodo's spin is an even stronger visual hook.
2. **First-person POV / "this fixed my task paralysis"** — peer testimonial > explainer [neurolaunch.com, n.d.]; mirrors Finch's relatable, messy, anti-perfection brand film ("eating cake from the fridge, rage-cleaning") [chiefmarketer.com, n.d.].
3. **"Apps that fixed my ADHD" listicle / before-after** — high-saturation but high-reach; entry depends on getting into creator roundups (the Inflow/Goblin Tools discovery path) [getinflow.io, 2026].

**Creator/influencer angle & the paid-vs-organic truth.** The proven playbook is the **Finch model**: ~$30M ARR with zero VC, built largely via Reddit/Discord/Facebook communities *plus heavy paid TikTok/Meta* [blog.sparrowapps.io, 2025]. The honest tell: even Finch's "organic-looking" growth is **paid-driven** — budget for paid social; do not assume free virality. **Cadence: no source gave a hard number** — standard niche practice is high-frequency short-form (several/week to daily), but treat that as a hypothesis to test *(inferred)*.

| **"decision paralysis app"** | Recognized concept, **lower competition** *(inferred)* | In copy but rarely *branded* around. **WheelTodo's strongest ownable niche term** — high intent, on-mechanic [morgen.so, 2026] |
| "task roulette" | Low volume, direct-match competitors | Two "Task Roulette" apps exist; ownable but tiny — brand/long-tail, not a traffic driver |
| "Pomodoro wheel" | Near-zero competition *and* near-zero volume *(inferred)* | No product owns it; grab defensively / novelty PR, not traffic [focuskeeper.co, 2026] |
| "focus timer" / "Pomodoro" | Very high volume, very high competition | Focus To-Do, Pomofocus, Forest dominate. Compete on long-tail ("Pomodoro for ADHD") [zapier.com, 2026] |
| "gentle productivity" / "anti-hustle" / "kind productivity" | Low ASO volume, high editorial/SEO value *(inferred)* | Few apps brand on these (Llama Life is the notable one) — strong for content/SEO + differentiation, weak as raw store-search drivers [focusbear.io, 2026] |
| "body doubling app" | Rising, mid competition *(inferred)* | Real trend (Dubbii, Focusmate). Only tangential until WheelTodo ships the companion |

**ASO recommendation:** Anchor the App Store title/subtitle on the **high-intent, lower-competition cluster — "decision paralysis," "ADHD focus timer," "ADHD to-do."** Use "gentle / anti-hustle / Pomodoro wheel / task roulette" as long-tail metadata + content/SEO. Note: for ADHD audiences specifically, the icon/screenshots must convert in seconds with low cognitive load — keywords are only half the battle [moburst.com, 2026].

**The "rest is productive" cultural tailwind.** This is a real, named movement with identifiable owners: **"Rest Is Resistance"** (Tricia Hersey / Nap Ministry, 2022 — powerful but politically loaded, cite as inspiration not brand claim) [hachettebookgroup.com, 2022]; **"soft life"** (Nigerian-origin Gen-Z trend, now in therapy discourse) [growtherapy.com, n.d.]; and **"gentle productivity"** (coined by the Work Brighter blog, modeled on gentle parenting — "getting things done without treating yourself horribly") [workbrighter.co, n.d.]. **No single dominant brand owns "gentle productivity" — it's up for grabs.**

## 5. Strategic recommendations for the WheelTodo beta launch

1. **Positioning line:** *"The kind to-do app that decides for you — and lets you rest without losing your streak."* Lead with two ownable wedges: **(a) the wheel kills decision paralysis** (spin → one task → focus, in one tap) and **(b) Rest Mode makes rest count.** Do NOT claim to have invented the wheel — claim the *integration + the gentleness*.

2. **Define against exactly three competitors:**
   - **Habitica** — the punishment foil. "Your avatar doesn't die when you rest." Weakness: HP-loss/streak-pressure mechanic that critics say "creates anxiety instead of motivation" [calmevo.com, 2026].
   - **Finch** — the gentle benchmark to differentiate *within*. Finch makes self-care the loop but has no task-execution engine and no decision mechanic; WheelTodo adds "actually start the task." Weakness: it's a wellness companion, not a get-the-thing-done tool.
   - **Llama Life / Tiimo** — the closest ADHD-tonal cousins. Both are gentle and per-task-timed but **both still require you to decide what to start.** Weakness: no decision-paralysis solver; WheelTodo removes the choice.

3. **Single most ownable wedge:** **"Rest protects your streak."** It is genuinely unoccupied (every competitor treats rest as an exemption, not a positive action), it's on-trend (gentle productivity / soft life / rest-is-resistance), and it's mechanically unique to WheelTodo (Rest Mode + energy check-in). This should be the hero of the launch deck, not the wheel.

4. **Channel & content:** Build the short-form engine around the **satisfying spin-loop demo** + **first-person "this fixed my task paralysis" POV** + **Rest Mode reveal** ("the app that *rewards* you for napping"). Subtitle everything; lead with peer voice, not features [neurolaunch.com, n.d.]. **Budget for paid TikTok/Meta from day one** — Finch proves even category-leading "organic" growth is paid-driven [blog.sparrowapps.io, 2025].

5. **Riskiest assumption:** That **the wheel is a durable daily-retention mechanic and not a one-time novelty.** Decision wheels are a saturated *novelty* category — the open question is whether spinning stays satisfying past week one, or whether users churn once the gimmick wears off and only the (less differentiated) Pomodoro remains. Mitigation: make **Rest Mode + streak**, not the wheel, the retention spine; instrument week-1→week-4 spin frequency and rest-day usage in beta as the primary signal. Secondary risk: "gentle/anti-hustle" ADHD positioning already has strong incumbents (Tiimo, Finch, Llama Life), so the wedge *must* be the rest-streak inversion, not gentleness in the abstract.
