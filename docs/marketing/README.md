# WheelTodo — Marketing

> Status: in-progress · beta-launch collateral (authored 2026-06-23)

Positioning, research, and go-to-market collateral for the WheelTodo beta launch.

**The hero of the launch is "Rest protects your streak" — not the wheel.** Every competitor treats rest as an exemption (streak shields, vacation mode); WheelTodo is the only one where resting is a positive move that keeps your streak alive.

## Honesty flags (carried through every deliverable)

- **The wheel mechanic is not novel** — two apps are literally named "Task Roulette." We claim the *integration* (wheel → one task → auto-Pomodoro → Rest Mode) and the gentleness, never invention.
- **Body-double companion is roadmap, not shipped** — the `apps/mobile/assets/body-double/` loops are not wired into the app yet. Tease only.
- **ASO volumes are inferred** — exact figures are paywalled in ASO tools; treat as directional.

## Define against

**Habitica** (punishes you) · **Finch** (gentle but can't execute tasks) · **Llama Life / Tiimo** (gentle but still make you choose).
